Files in this repository: public/models/creatures/rat.glb (the Rat body, without its clips) and the Rat's clips
in public/models/animations/rat/, one clip per file, built from FBX/Rat.fbx by converting it to GLB in Blender
and then running tools/build-creature.ts.

The Spider is from the same pack: public/models/creatures/spider.glb and its clips in
public/models/animations/spider/, built from FBX/Spider.fbx the same way.
