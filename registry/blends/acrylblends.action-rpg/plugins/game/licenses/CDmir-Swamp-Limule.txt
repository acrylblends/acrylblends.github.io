Files in this repository: public/models/creatures/crawler.glb (the body, without its clips) and the clips in
public/models/animations/crawler/, one clip per file, built from limule-final.blend by exporting it to GLB in
Blender and then running tools/build-creature.ts with --flat. The textures packed in the .blend are not shipped:
the body is one flat colour.
